# Weather Frame watercolor scene prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-screen 1600×1200 e-ink weather artwork

Primary request:
Create an original watercolor landscape of **{environment_name}** during **{weather_name}**.

Geography:
{environment_description}. Make the location recognizable through its large
landforms and geology. Do not invent a famous landmark that is not part of this
place. If an attached geography reference is present, use it only for landform
identity, proportions, vegetation, and viewpoint—not for photographic style.

Weather:
{weather_description}. Weather must visibly affect the sky, atmosphere,
vegetation, ground, water, snow cover, visibility, and color temperature. It
must feel physically integrated into this location, not pasted on top.

Style:
Match the attached positive STYLE reference closely: luminous transparent
watercolor washes on subtly textured cold-pressed paper; layered wet-on-wet
skies; confident broken-edged brush shapes; rich but natural pigment variation;
detailed enough to identify terrain and vegetation, but unmistakably painted.
Use the reference for medium, brushwork, edge softness, pigment granulation,
detail density, and atmosphere only. Do not copy its exact mountains, tree
placement, or cloud arrangement.

E-ink color design:
Build the image around six strong pigment families that survive Spectra-6
quantization: warm paper cream, deep indigo/charcoal, earthy red, golden ochre,
clear blue, and forest green. Use large readable value groups and decisive
silhouettes. Preserve meaningful contrast between sky, distant landforms,
middle ground, and foreground. Avoid muddy all-gray passages and extremely
subtle gradients.

Composition:
Wide 4:3 landscape, full bleed to every edge, no white margin. One coherent
viewpoint with deep foreground, middle ground, recognizable main landform, and
weather-dominant sky. Keep important peaks and weather features away from the
outermost 3% so display rotation cannot clip them. No split panels or collage.

Constraints:
No people, animals, vehicles, buildings, roads, signs, flags, lettering, map
labels, captions, borders, frames, signatures, logos, or watermarks. No
photorealism, 3D rendering, vector art, flat clip art, paint-by-number shapes,
anime, or fantasy geography. Do not imitate a named living artist.
